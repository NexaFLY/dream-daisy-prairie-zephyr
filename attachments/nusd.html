Nexa USD (nUSD)

Nexa USD (nUSD) is the internal stable token of the Nexa (FLY) ecosystem.
Designed to facilitate exchanges, donations, and liquidity pool participation, nUSD allows users to easily access the ecosystem while maintaining a stable value relative to USDC.

nUSD is flexible and controlled by Nexa (FLY) to ensure security and stability, with the possibility to evolve towards new features in the future.

Backed 1:1 with USDC
