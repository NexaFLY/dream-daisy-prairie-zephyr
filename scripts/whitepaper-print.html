Introduction

Nexa is a French non-profit organization (Law 1901) designed to unite multiple charitable associations under a single ecosystem. Its mission is to make crypto donations transparent, secure, and efficient using blockchain technology.

By leveraging Solana’s high-speed, low-fee network, Nexa aims to modernize philanthropy and expand crypto adoption in the charitable sector. Every contribution should be traceable, verifiable, and attributed — without relying on an opaque intermediary.

02

Platform

Project overview

Nexa provides a decentralized platform where associations receive cryptocurrency donations without intermediaries. The FLY token is the utility token of the ecosystem: donors can track contributions in real time and take part in governance. This approach builds trust, transparency, and community involvement in charitable giving.

nexafly.org is the public front of that infrastructure: an association directory, USDC → FLY swap (Jupiter, Titan), the internal dollar nUSD, and a treasury wallet anyone can inspect.

Path of a donation

1

Enter the rail

The donor swaps USDC (or SOL) into FLY, or operates in nUSD. The swap runs on Solana DEXs — Jupiter or Titan — not inside a private black box.

2

Send to a public wallet

Funds go to Nexa FLY’s transparency wallet, or to an association’s own wallet once it has been claimed. The address is published. The transaction is visible on Solscan.

3

Tag, then redistribute

Hosted associations first share the Nexa treasury. Each gift is tagged in the ledger (destination association). Redistribution to beneficiaries remains on-chain.

Principle

Trust is not declared. It is checked — on an explorer, against a public address, without an unreadable PDF report six months later.

03

FLY

Token utility

FLY is not a security. It is the association’s utility token: a rail for donations, governance, liquidity, and incentives.

Donations

Direct contribution

FLY can be used for direct gifts to registered associations. The transfer is a public, timestamped Solana transaction.

Governance

Holder votes

Token holders vote on ecosystem proposals and partnership decisions. Votes are visible on-chain.

Liquidity

Tradable on DEXs

FLY trades on Solana DEXs (Raydium, Orca) and can be bought via Jupiter or Titan from USDC or SOL.

Incentives

Participation

Community members and partner associations may receive FLY for active participation.

04

Internal stable

nUSD, the dollar on the FLY rail

Nexa USD (nUSD) is the internal stable token, pegged 1:1 to USDC. It is used for gifts, pools, and association treasury without exposing the donor to FLY price risk. USDC remains the external rail — the site’s public book reads the FLY / USDC pair, the deepest market.

Network

Ecosystem & partnerships

Nexa collaborates with verified non-profit organizations so donations reach legitimate beneficiaries. Multiple Solana DEXs carry liquidity. Phantom and Solflare make FLY accessible worldwide.

Hosted associations

Major French and international associations are pre-listed in the directory (Red Cross, MSF, UNICEF, WWF, Restos du Cœur, and others). Until an association claims its own wallet, gifts intended for it transit through the Nexa FLY treasury — the public wallet below — and are tagged in the ledger. That balance is not “theirs”: it is a deposit awaiting redistribution, under Nexa’s control.
